# Bødekasse

A shared fine box for a group of friends: members fine each other for breaking agreed rules, and the money goes into the box. Danish terms are canonical; English equivalents are listed for reference.

## Language

### Box and people

**Bødekasse** (Box):
One group's fine box: its members, its Bødekategorier, its settings, and the money owed to it. A person can belong to several.
_Avoid_: Group, team, kasse

**Medlem** (Member):
A person in a Bødekasse. May exist as name + email before the person has signed up; signing in with that email claims the spot.
_Avoid_: User, player, participant

**Udmeldt** (Removed):
A former Medlem an Admin has taken out of the Bødekasse. Their history and any unpaid Saldo stay in the Bødekasse, and an Admin can bring them back.
_Avoid_: Slettet, inaktiv, forladt

**Admin**:
A Medlem who can change a Bødekasse's settings, manage Medlemmer, and register Indbetalinger. The creator is the first Admin.
_Avoid_: Kasserer, owner, moderator

**Sæson** (Season):
A period in which a Bødekasse is active: Medlemmer give Bøder and Gebyrer arise. An Admin starts it and it ends on its end date or when an Admin ends it; unpaid Saldo carries over into the next one.
_Avoid_: Season, periode, år

**Pause** (Break):
The time between one Sæson ending and the next starting. No Bøder or Gebyrer arise, but Indbetalinger and withdrawals still happen.
_Avoid_: Sæsonpause, break

### Charges

**Bødekategori** (Rule):
The definition of a fine: a name and a price. Any Medlem can create one.
_Avoid_: Regel, fine type

**Bøde** (Fine):
A fine one Medlem gives another, either from a Bødekategori or as an Engangsbøde. Keeps the price it had when it was given.
_Avoid_: Penalty, charge

**Engangsbøde** (One-off fine):
A Bøde given with a free-text reason and amount instead of a Bødekategori. The giver is charged a Gebyr for it.
_Avoid_: Custom Bøde, ad-hoc Bøde

**Gebyr** (Fee):
A charge the Bødekasse imposes itself rather than one Medlem giving another, e.g. for creating a Bødekategori, giving an Engangsbøde, filing an Anke, or placing in a Konkurrence.
_Avoid_: Afgift, ankebøde

**Frikøb** (Buy-out):
A one-off payment that exempts a Medlem from Bøder for the rest of the Sæson. Never covers Gebyrer.
_Avoid_: Amnesti, amnesty

**Anke** (Appeal):
A Medlem's challenge of a Bøde they received. Filing one always costs a Gebyr (a random amount between 1 kr and twice the Bøde's price). The other Medlemmer decide the outcome; whoever loses bears the Bøde, so a won Anke moves it to the Medlem who gave it.
_Avoid_: Klage, dispute

**Konkurrence** (Competition):
A contest among a Bødekasse's Medlemmer, held whenever the group likes. Each placement results in a Gebyr, and every Medlem who didn't take part gets an absent Gebyr.
_Avoid_: Game, spil

### Money

**Indbetaling** (Payment):
Money a Medlem has paid into the Bødekasse.
_Avoid_: Transaction, settlement

**Saldo** (Balance):
What a Medlem currently owes: their Bøder and Gebyrer minus their Indbetalinger.
_Avoid_: Debt, gæld
